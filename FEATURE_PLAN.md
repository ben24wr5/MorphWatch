# Morph Watch — Feature Plan

Everything picked so far, grouped into build stages. ✅ = built.

## Rules
- Every new item and block must be craftable. **Ask for the recipe before building each one.**
- The watch has a gold face and a red strap.

## Recipes (3x3 crafting table)

**Morph Watch** ✅
```
Gold Block   Red Wool     Gold Block
Gold Block   Ender Pearl  Gold Block
Gold Block   Red Wool     Gold Block
```

**Recharge Crystal** (refills your powers instantly)
```
TNT          Red Wool     TNT
Red Wool     Redstone     Red Wool
TNT          Red Wool     TNT
```

**Watch Workbench** (upgrade and style the watch)
```
Green Wool   Black Wool   Green Wool
Black Wool   TNT          Black Wool
Green Wool   Black Wool   Green Wool
```

**Mob Statue**: no preference given yet. Default plan:
```
(empty)       Smooth Stone  (empty)
Smooth Stone  Armor Stand   Smooth Stone
Smooth Stone  Smooth Stone  Smooth Stone
```

**Diamond Morph Watch** ✅: Morph Watch with a diamond above, below, left and right.
**Netherite Morph Watch** ✅: smithing table (template + Diamond Morph Watch + netherite ingot).

Still need recipes for: DNA cards, gems, watch trophies, and any other new item.

## Stage 1 ✅ (built, version 2.0.0)
- 12 mobs: Chicken, Cat, Creeper, Spider, Zombie, Skeleton, Blaze, Enderman, Wither Skeleton, Iron Golem, Bat, Snow Golem
- Unlock by scanning, no time limit
- Scan beam, mob menu, on-screen icon, ready beep, glow when ready, flash and sound, mob sounds
- Disguise, mob friends, auto-escape, mob's real health
- Two powers per mob, charged power, mob attacks
- Diamond and Netherite upgrades, golden forms, achievements

## Stage 2 ✅: the dial and transformation (built, version 2.1.0)
- **X** pops up the dial, the **scroll wheel** turns it left and right, **C** slams the watch and transforms
- The dial only shows scanned mobs
- A hologram silhouette of the mob rises out of the watch, so friends can see it too
- Transformation: a gold light burst, a spin, shrinking or growing to the mob's size, and the camera zooming to third person and back
- Dial click sounds, golden forms glow on the dial, screen shake on the slam
- Transform jingle, fast transform (upgraded watches), transform cooldown
- (X and C are also Minecraft's creative hotbar save and load keys, but those only trigger together with a number key.)

## Stage 3: watch style and items
- Watch faces (classic, digital, alien), glow colours, case shapes (round, square, hexagon), engraving
- Strap colours, glow in the dark, watch nickname
- Watch Workbench, Recharge Crystal, Mob Statue
- Gem slots, DNA cards, watch battery, lost watch tracker, watch lock

## Stage 4: watch gadgets and helpers
- Flashlight, danger alarm, home compass, weather forecast
- Mob radar, scan info card
- Tutorial, auto-human when you sleep
- Power voice lines, sound settings
- Friendly fire off, stuck fix

## Stage 5: mob life
- Mob armor, mob colours, mob walk sounds, mob voice chat, mob emotes
- Mob talk, mob homes, mob food
- Block carrying (Enderman), mob mining, Blaze cooking, swim forms
- Day/night powers, weather powers

## Stage 6: levelling and big stuff
- Mob levels, skill points, daily challenge, shiny mobs
- Combo hits, boss forms, fusion forms
- Watch trophies, watch link (see friends who wear watches)
